# Eazo Share Widgets

## App
Premise: 隔空魔方 / Air Cube — a phone-held 3D Rubik's cube you view with gyroscope parallax and rotate/twist with rear-camera hand gestures (touch twist as reliable fallback). Solve a scrambled 3x3, tracked by time and move count.
Visual identity: FUI broadcast layer — electric-blue #1a3fd4 field with a fine dot-grid, frosted glass HUD chips, orange #FF8C00 primary and gold #FFD700 / green #00FF88 accents, monospace technical labels.

## Scenario: cube_solve
Available facts: solve time (mm:ss.cs), move count, control mode (gesture|touch)
Composition: lead with the big solve time as the dominant tabular-numeral hero; support with move count and mode as small monospace chips; a small floating cube glyph as motif.
Visual cues: palette electric-blue ground with orange/gold/green FUI accents; type bold geometric sans for the time + monospace uppercase micro-labels; motif = frosted glass chip edges + dot-grid overlay + a single glowing green tracking dot.
